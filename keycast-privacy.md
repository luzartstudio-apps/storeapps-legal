# KeyCast — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

KeyCast ("the app") is a Windows app that shows the keys and mouse clicks you press on your screen, for screen recordings and presentations.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and it never connects to the internet.

**Keystrokes and clicks:** to show them on screen, the app watches keyboard and mouse input on your PC while it is running. Each key or click is turned into an on-screen bubble the moment it happens and disappears after a few seconds. Keystrokes are never written to disk, never logged and never sent anywhere. The app never blocks or changes your input.

**"KeyCast" tag:** by default, a small "KeyCast" label is drawn under the keys so viewers know which app shows them. It is only drawn on your screen, contains no information about you, is never added to any file, and can be turned off with one click in the app (free and Pro versions).

**Password fields:** the app checks whether the focused element is a password field (using Windows UI Automation) and shows nothing while you type in it. This works for standard password fields that Windows can identify; terminal windows, remote desktop sessions and some games cannot be detected, so pause the app before typing passwords there. If password fields cannot be detected at all on your PC, the app tells you. Text typed automatically by other programs, such as password managers, is ignored. You can also pause the app at any time from the notification area.

**Local data:** your display settings (including whether the "KeyCast" tag is shown), a count of completed sessions used to time the optional rating request, and a small diagnostic log are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log never contains keystrokes. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
