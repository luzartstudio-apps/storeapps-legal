# PauseBreak — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

PauseBreak ("the app") is a Windows app that reminds you to rest your eyes and to stand up and stretch at regular intervals.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and it never connects to the internet.

**Keyboard and mouse:** the app does not install keyboard or mouse hooks and never reads what you type. To know whether you are at the PC and whether you are in the middle of typing, it only asks Windows how long it has been since the last input (GetLastInputInfo) and whether a mouse button is currently held down. Break screens never take keyboard focus.

**Quiet mode signals:** to stay quiet at the right moments, the app reads — and never changes — a few Windows states on your PC: whether an app is running full screen or you are presenting, whether Do not disturb / Focus Assist is on, and the size and program name of the window in front so it can tell you why it is staying quiet. These are used immediately and are not stored.

**Microphone and camera (Pro):** when "Stay quiet during calls" is turned on, the app checks whether another app is currently using your microphone or camera by reading the usage timestamps that Windows keeps in your privacy settings. The app never opens, records or listens to the microphone, never opens the camera, and needs no microphone or camera permission.

**Screen sharing:** break screens are marked so that Windows hides them from screen sharing and screen recordings.

**Local data:** your settings, a count of the breaks you took each day (kept for 90 days) and a small diagnostic log are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log contains only app events (for example, "break started"), never anything you type. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
