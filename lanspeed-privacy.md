# Lantide — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Lantide ("the app") is a Windows app that measures the speed of your home network (Wi-Fi or LAN) between your PC and a phone or other device that opens the test page in its web browser.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**How the test works:** while the app is open, it runs a small web server on your PC that listens only on your private network addresses (TCP ports 38500 to 38509). The test page is built into the app. It opens only for devices on your private network that have the random session code shown in the app's QR code; the code changes every time the app starts or you click "New code". During a test, the phone and the PC exchange random test data to measure download, upload and latency. This test data is thrown away right after it is counted and is never saved or sent anywhere else.

**What the app does not do:** it does not connect to the internet, does not measure or report your internet speed to anyone, and does not scan, list or contact other devices on your network. It does not read your files.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings, your test results (date and time, the location name you typed such as "Bedroom", download and upload speed, latency, jitter and lost pings) and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). The phone's browser remembers the last location name you typed, on the phone only. The log never contains the session code, file names or test results. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your PC. If you choose to export your results (Pro), the CSV file is saved only where you choose.

**Purchases:** purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
