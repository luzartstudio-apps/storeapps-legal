# Twirlwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Twirlwell ("the app") is a Windows app that spins a wheel to pick an entry from your list at random and splits lists into random groups.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your lists and files:** the names and other entries you type or paste, your saved lists, the history of results and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own folder in your Windows user profile (AppData). When you import a list, the app only reads the TXT or CSV file you choose or drop; the file itself is never changed. When you export the history or groups (Pro), the CSV file is written only where you choose to save it. Pictures you add to slices (Pro) are copied as small square images into the app's own folder; your original pictures are never changed. You can clear the history and delete saved lists inside the app at any time.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not send anything over the network. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** besides your lists, the app stores its settings and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes all of them. The log never contains entries from your lists, file names or folder paths.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Teachers may type students' first names into a list; these names stay on the teacher's device.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
