# SnipClip — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

SnipClip ("the app") is a Windows app that cuts, trims and joins video files on your computer without re-encoding them.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app only reads the videos you open, drop into its window or open with it from File Explorer. It reads the video's index and copies the parts you choose into a new file, which is saved next to the original (for example "name_cut.mp4" or "name_audio.m4a"). Your original video is never changed. Information stored inside your video, such as the recording date or location your phone or camera wrote into it, is kept in the new video file as it is (audio-only .m4a files leave it out). No video, file name or metadata is ever uploaded. The preview uses the video player built into Windows on your device.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not connect to the internet. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings (including how many free exports were used today) and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains file names, folder paths or file contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
