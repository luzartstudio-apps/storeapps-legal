# Spanplot — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Spanplot ("the app") is a Windows app for building Gantt charts: you enter your own tasks, dates and dependencies and print or export the schedule.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your projects:** everything you type (project and task names, dates, owners, progress) is saved only on this PC, in the .gantt file you choose. If you type people's names as task owners, those names stay in your file.

**Files you choose:** the app reads or writes a file only when you ask it to: opening or saving a .gantt project, or (Pro) importing or exporting a CSV file and exporting a PDF or PNG. Printing sends the chart only to the printer you pick. The app does not read any other files.

**Network:** the app does not need or use an internet connection. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings, a temporary copy of your unsaved changes (deleted when you save or discard them) and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. Your .gantt files are not removed. The log never contains task names, file names or folder paths: only counts and error codes.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
