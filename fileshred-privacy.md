# Wipewell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Wipewell ("the app") is a Windows app that permanently deletes the files and folders you choose by overwriting their contents before deleting them, and (Pro) overwrites the free space of a drive.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app only works on the files and folders you add yourself (by dragging them in, choosing them in a dialog or pasting them). It reads their names, sizes and attributes to show the list, and when you confirm, it overwrites their contents on this PC, renames them to random names and deletes them. This is permanent by design: deleted files do not go to the Recycle Bin and cannot be restored by the app or by us. The app refuses Windows system folders, program folders, whole drives, the main user folders themselves and its own folders, and it never follows shortcuts, symbolic links or junctions to other places. Nothing from your files is sent anywhere.

**Free space wiping (Pro):** when you choose a drive, the app writes temporary files filled with random data into its own hidden folder on that drive (on the drive that holds your user profile, inside the app's data folder), then deletes them. It never reads or changes your other files. If the app is closed unexpectedly, it removes any leftover temporary files the next time it starts.

**Deletion reports (Pro):** if you save a report, it is written only to the place you choose. It contains the date, the method used and the names and locations of the items you deleted or that were skipped. The app does not keep a copy.

**Limits you should know:** on SSDs, USB drives and memory cards, the drive's own wear leveling and TRIM can keep old copies of data in areas no app can reach; using BitLocker (device encryption) protects against this. Files in OneDrive or other cloud-synced folders may still have copies or older versions in the cloud, and network drives may keep their own copies. File system journals, restore points and backups made by other software are not affected by the app.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and never sends file names or any other information anywhere. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings (chosen method, daily usage count of the free version, last chosen drive) and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains file names, folder paths or drive labels — only counts, sizes, durations and error codes.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
