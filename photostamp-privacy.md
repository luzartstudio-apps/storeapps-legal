# Ownmark — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Ownmark ("the app") is a Windows app that adds your text, a logo, or the date a photo was taken to copies of the photos you choose.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your photos:** the app only opens the photos you drop, paste, pick in the file dialog, open with the app, or add as a folder. Photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. Each result is saved as a new file in a folder named "Ownmark" next to the original; the original file is never changed. Photos can contain information such as the date, camera details and location (GPS). The app reads the date taken and rotation from the photo. In the free version this information is not copied into the new files; in Pro, if you turn on "Keep photo info", it is copied into the new JPEG files, and you decide whether to share them.

**Your logo, text and settings:** the watermark text, settings and saved templates are stored only on your device, in the app's folder in your Windows user profile (AppData). If you choose a logo (Pro), a copy of that PNG file is kept in the same folder so your templates keep working if you move the file; your original logo is not changed.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not send anything over the network. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Microphone, camera and keyboard:** the app does not use the microphone or camera and does not monitor your typing.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings, a daily count of free stamps and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes all of them. The log never contains photo content, watermark text, file names or folder paths.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
