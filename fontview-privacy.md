# Glyphwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Glyphwell ("the app") is a Windows app for previewing, comparing and organizing the fonts on your computer, including font files you have not installed yet.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app reads font files (.ttf, .otf, .ttc) only on your device: the fonts installed in Windows, the font folders you add, and the font files you drop, paste or open with it. It reads the font's own information (family and style names, version, designer, license text, which characters it contains) to show previews. Font files are never changed, moved or deleted by the app. When you choose Install, the app asks Windows (File Explorer) to install that font for your user account, exactly like right-clicking the file and choosing Install; Windows copies the font into your personal font folder. When you use "Activate for now" (Pro), the font is made available to other apps only until you turn it off or close the app; nothing is copied or installed. Sample sheets are printed or saved only where you choose. Text you type to preview fonts stays on your device.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not send anything over the network. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings in your Windows user profile (AppData): the list of font folders you added, your favorites and collections (font names only), your preview text and size, a small list of fonts that are temporarily active (so they can be removed if the app was closed unexpectedly), and temporary copies of the not-yet-installed fonts you preview (so your original files are never kept open; they are deleted the next time the app starts). It also keeps a small diagnostic log that never contains file names, folder paths or the text you type. Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
