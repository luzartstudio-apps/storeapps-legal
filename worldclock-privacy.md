# Zonewell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Zonewell ("the app") is a Windows app that shows the local time of the cities you choose and converts times between time zones.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store on our servers, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your data:** the cities you add, the names you give to clocks and the working hours you enter are stored only on your device, in the app's own folder in your Windows user profile (AppData). If you type a person's name for a clock, it stays on your device. The app does not read your files, contacts or calendar.

**Location:** the app does not use your location. The time shown as "your time" comes from the clock and time zone setting of your PC.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not send anything over the network. The city table is built into the app and daylight saving rules come from Windows. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Microphone, camera, screen and keyboard:** the app does not use the microphone or camera, does not capture the screen and does not monitor your typing.

**Local data:** besides the items above, the app stores its settings and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains city names, the names you typed or file names.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
