# Ladlewell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Ladlewell ("the app") is a Windows recipe and food cost calculator: you type in ingredients and what you paid for them, build recipes, and the app calculates cost per serving and a suggested menu price. It calculates costs only and gives no nutrition or health information and no tax advice.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your recipe data:** everything you enter (ingredient names, prices, pack sizes, waste percentages, recipes, servings, menu prices and the price history the app keeps when you change a price) is saved only on this PC, in your Windows user profile (AppData).

**Files you choose:** the app writes a file only when you ask it to export to Excel or PDF (Pro), and only to the location you pick. It does not open or read any other files of yours.

**Network:** the app does not need or use an internet connection. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** besides your recipe book and its previous version, the app keeps its settings and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). If the recipe file is ever damaged, it is kept next to the book under a new name rather than overwritten. Uninstalling the app removes all of this. The log never contains ingredient names, recipe names, prices, file names or folder paths: only counts and error codes.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
