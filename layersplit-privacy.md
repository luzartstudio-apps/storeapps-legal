# LayerSplit — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

LayerSplit ("the app") is a Windows app that splits a flat image into separate layers (sky, depth planes, character parts) for editing and animation.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no telemetry, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your images:** the images you open are processed entirely on your PC by AI models that are included in the app (depth estimation, subject segmentation, pose estimation, region selection and image fill). They are never uploaded or shared. The app only reads your images; it never changes, moves or deletes them. Layers are saved only where you choose (PSD file or a folder of PNG files).

**AI-generated content:** when "Fill hidden areas" is on, the app uses an AI model to paint the parts of each layer that were hidden behind other layers. These painted areas are created by AI and may not match what was really there. If you see AI-generated content that is inappropriate, use "Report AI content" in the app or write to the address below.

**Network:** the app works fully offline. Its only network use is the Microsoft Store checking your Pro license.

**Local data:** your settings and a small diagnostic log are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log never contains file names, paths or image content, only counts, timings and error codes. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
