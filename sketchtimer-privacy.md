# Posebeat — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Posebeat ("the app") is a Windows app that shows reference images from a folder you choose, one at a time with a countdown, for timed figure and gesture drawing practice.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store on our servers, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app does not include or download any images. It only reads the image files in the folders you add (with the Add folder button or by dropping a folder on the window), to show them on screen during practice. Your images are never changed, moved, copied or uploaded. The app remembers the paths of the folders you added, the labels you give them, your classes and your settings, so you can use them again; you can remove a folder from the list at any time (the folder itself stays on your PC).

**Practice log:** the app keeps a small log on your PC with, for each day you practiced, the number of poses and the minutes you drew. It contains no image names, folder names or pictures.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not send anything over the network. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Microphone, camera, screen and keyboard:** the app does not use the microphone or camera, does not record the screen and does not monitor your typing. Keyboard shortcuts work only while the app window is in front.

**Local data:** the folder list, settings, classes, practice log and a small diagnostic log are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The diagnostic log never contains file names, folder paths or image contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
