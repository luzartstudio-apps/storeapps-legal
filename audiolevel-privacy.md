# Levelwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Levelwell ("the app") is a Windows app that converts audio files to other formats and adjusts their loudness to a standard level (LUFS). It also measures the loudness of audio files.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app only opens the audio or video files you choose (with the file picker, by dropping or pasting them, or with "Open with"). It reads them on your PC to measure their loudness and to create a converted copy. The new file is saved next to the original as "name (levelwell).ext", or in a folder you choose (Pro). Your original files are never modified, moved or deleted. When "Keep title, artist and album" is on (Pro), the app copies these song details from the original into the new file. The loudness report (Pro) is only shown in the app, copied to your clipboard or saved as a CSV file where you choose. File names and file contents are never sent anywhere.

**Network:** the app does not need or use an internet connection. Audio is decoded and encoded with the codecs built into Windows, on your PC. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings (chosen format, quality, loudness target, output folder, and the number of free files used today) and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains file names, folder paths or file contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
