# ClearMatte — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

ClearMatte ("the app") is a Windows app that removes the background from images on your PC.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no telemetry, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your images:** the images you open are processed entirely on your PC by AI models that are included in the app. They are never uploaded or shared. The app only reads your images; it never changes, moves or deletes them. Results are saved only where you choose (by default next to the original, as "name-nobg.png"), or copied to the clipboard when you click Copy.

**Network:** removing backgrounds works with Wi-Fi turned off. The app's network use is limited to the Microsoft Store checking your Pro license, and — only if you turn on "High quality" mode and the model is not yet on your PC — a one-time download of that model file (about 224 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) over HTTPS. That download is a normal web request for a fixed public file; it never includes your images or any other personal data. You can remove the downloaded model at any time in Settings.

**Local data:** your settings and a small diagnostic log are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log never contains file names, paths or image content, only counts, timings and error codes. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
