# Sealwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Sealwell ("the app") is a Windows app that encrypts folders you choose into password-protected vault files and restores them when you provide the password.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store on our servers, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app reads the folders and files you add so it can encrypt them, reads the vault files you open, and writes vault files and restored folders to the places you choose. If you ask it to, it moves your original folder to the Recycle Bin or deletes it, only after the vault has been fully checked. Everything happens on your PC. File contents, file names, passwords and keyfiles are never sent anywhere.

**Passwords and keyfiles:** your password and keyfile are used only in memory to unlock a vault. They are never stored, logged or transmitted, and we have no way to recover them. If you forget your password, the data in a vault cannot be recovered by anyone.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not send your data anywhere. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Settings include the locations of vaults you have opened and not yet locked again, so the app can offer "Lock again", and the choices you made. Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains file names, passwords or file contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
