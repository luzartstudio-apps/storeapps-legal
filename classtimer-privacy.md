# Chalktick — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Chalktick ("the app") is a Windows app that shows a large classroom timer and exam countdown clock, for use on a projector or second screen.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store on our servers, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your data:** the exam subjects and times, lesson and activity names, group names and settings you enter are stored only on your device, in the app's own folder in your Windows user profile (AppData). If you use the Pro features to add a background photo or an alarm sound, the app reads only the file you choose in the standard Open dialog and copies it into that same folder; your original file is never changed. You can remove the photo or sound in the app at any time.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not send anything over the network. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Microphone, camera, screen and keyboard:** the app does not use the microphone or camera, does not record the screen and does not monitor your typing. Keyboard shortcuts work only while the app window is in front.

**Local data:** besides the items above, the app stores its settings and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes all of them. The log never contains exam subjects, names, file names or folder paths.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. A teacher may type group names into the app; these stay on the teacher's device.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
