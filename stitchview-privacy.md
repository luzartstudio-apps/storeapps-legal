# Hoopwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Hoopwell ("the app") is a Windows app that opens machine embroidery design files (DST, EXP, JEF, PES), shows their stitches, and can convert them to other embroidery formats.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app reads only the embroidery files and folders you open. Designs are read and displayed on your PC and are never uploaded. Files you convert or export (Pro) are saved where you choose or next to the original, under a new name; the app never changes or overwrites your original files. When you print a sheet, the page goes to the printer you select in Windows.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings (hoop size, chosen output folder, display options) and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains file names or file contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
