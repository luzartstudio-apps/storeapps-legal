# Padwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Padwell ("the app") is a Windows soundboard: it plays audio files you choose from on-screen buttons and global hotkeys.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app opens only the audio files you choose (with the file picker, drag and drop or paste). They are decoded and played on your PC using the audio codecs built into Windows; nothing is uploaded. The original files are never changed or moved. If you use Backup (Pro), the app writes a new backup file with copies of your sounds to the place you choose and never overwrites an existing file. If you restore a backup, the sounds in it are copied into the app's own folder in your Windows user profile.

**Boards and buttons:** the app saves your boards and each button's name, color, volume, hotkey, playback options and the location of its sound file in your Windows user profile (AppData), so they are there the next time you open the app. This stays on your PC.

**Hotkeys:** global hotkeys use the standard Windows RegisterHotKey function. The app is only told when one of your chosen key combinations is pressed. It does not read, record or store what you type.

**Microphone:** the app does not use the microphone and never opens a recording device. It only plays sound through the output devices on your PC.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings, your boards as described above and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains file names, folder names or file contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
