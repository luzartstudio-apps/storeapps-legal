# Hourwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Hourwell ("the app") is a Windows time tracker: you start and stop a timer for your clients and projects, and see your hours in a weekly timesheet.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store on our side, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your time records:** everything you enter (client and project names, hourly rates, session start and end times, notes and your weekly goal) is saved only on this PC, in a local database in your Windows user profile (AppData).

**Idle detection:** to notice when you step away while the timer is running, the app reads the system's "time of last input" (GetLastInputInfo). This tells the app only how long ago you last touched the mouse or keyboard. It does not capture keystrokes, mouse movements, window titles, or anything on your screen, and it stores nothing except the start and end of the time away that you choose to keep or remove. You can turn it off in Settings.

**Global shortcut:** the app registers one shortcut with Windows so you can start and stop the timer from any app. It receives only that shortcut, not other keys.

**Files you choose:** the app reads or writes a file only when you ask it to: exporting a CSV file, (Pro) exporting an Excel or PDF report, backing up or restoring your data. Your other files are never read or changed. Exports never overwrite an existing file.

**Network:** the app does not need or use an internet connection. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** besides your database, the app keeps its settings, a safety copy made before a restore, and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them, so back up first if you want to keep your hours. The log never contains client or project names, notes, file names or folder paths: only counts and error codes.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
