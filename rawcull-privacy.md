# Shutterwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Shutterwell ("the app") is a Windows app for reviewing photos: it shows the JPEG preview embedded in your RAW files, and lets you rate, pick and reject them.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app only reads the folders and files you open. It reads the embedded preview and the shooting details (such as camera, lens, exposure and date) from each photo. Those details stay on your PC and are never sent anywhere.

Your RAW and JPG files are never changed. When you rate, pick or reject a photo, the app writes a small XMP file next to it (for example IMG_0001.xmp) so other photo apps can read your rating; any information already in an existing XMP file is kept. If you use the optional Pro feature to copy or move photos, the files go only to the folder you choose. The app never deletes photos unless you choose to move them.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains file names or file contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
