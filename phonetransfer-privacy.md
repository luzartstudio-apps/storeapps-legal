# PicBeam — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

PicBeam ("the app") is a Windows app that receives photos, videos and other files from your phone over your local Wi‑Fi network.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**How transfers work:** while the app is open, it runs a small web server on your PC that listens only on your local (private) network. Your phone connects to it directly using the address in the QR code. Files travel only between your phone and your PC — they are never sent to the internet or to us. Each session uses a new random security code; requests without it are rejected. When you close the app, the server stops.

**Your files:** received files are saved only to the folder you choose on your PC (by default Pictures\PicBeam). If you use "Send to phone" (Pro), only the files you add are offered to the phone that scanned the code.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings, an optional transfer history (Pro) with file names and sizes, and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log does not contain the security code or file contents. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
