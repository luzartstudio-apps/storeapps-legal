# SubtitleCraft — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

SubtitleCraft ("the app") is a Windows app that creates subtitles for your videos with a speech recognition model that runs on your computer.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your videos, audio and subtitles:** files you open are read and processed only on your device. The app extracts the sound track into a temporary file in its local data folder, recognizes the speech on your PC and deletes the temporary file when you close the file or the app. Subtitles are saved only where you choose (by default next to your video). No video, audio, subtitle text, file name or metadata is ever uploaded.

**Network:** the app connects to the internet only to download the speech model you choose (57–547 MB), and only after you click the button that says so. Models are downloaded from huggingface.co (Hugging Face and its content delivery network see your IP address, as with any file download) and are checked against a fixed SHA-256 fingerprint. Once a model is downloaded, the app works fully offline. The "Report a problem with the text" link opens your own email app; nothing is sent automatically.

**AI-generated content:** subtitles are generated on your device by an AI speech recognition model (OpenAI Whisper weights run by whisper.cpp) and may contain mistakes. The app never sends your content to any AI service.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings, the downloaded speech models and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log never contains video, audio or subtitle text. You can delete models at any time in "Manage models". Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
