# MouseRemap — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

MouseRemap ("the app") is a Windows app that turns the extra buttons of a mouse into keyboard shortcuts.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and it never connects to the internet.

**Mouse buttons:** to do its job, the app watches mouse button presses on your PC. It only acts on the middle button and the two side buttons; the left and right buttons are always passed through unchanged. Button presses are handled in memory the moment they happen and are never recorded or saved. The app does not read what you type.

**Active program:** when you press a remapped button, the app reads the file name of the program in the active window (for example "chrome.exe") to pick the matching profile. This name is used only at that moment and is not stored, except for the programs you choose to add as profiles.

**Local data:** your button settings, the list of programs you added as profiles and a small diagnostic log are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log does not contain your clicks or keystrokes. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Start with Windows:** the app starts automatically when you sign in only if you turn this on in the app. You can turn it off in the app or in Windows Settings.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
