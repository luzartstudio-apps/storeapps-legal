# Pixsure — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Pixsure ("the app") is a Windows app that shows full-screen test patterns so you can check a monitor for dead pixels, uneven color and other visible problems.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your display:** the app reads your display's name, resolution, refresh rate and scale from Windows to show them to you. It reads the display name the screen reports, not its serial number. This information stays on your PC.

**Your files:** the app does not read your files. If you choose to save a report (Pro), it writes a PDF or PNG file only in the place you pick, and it never overwrites an existing file. The report contains the date, the display name, resolution, refresh rate and scale, the pixels you marked and the tests you viewed. It is yours to keep or to send to someone; the app does not send it anywhere.

**Screen contents:** the app does not capture, record or read what is on your screen.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings (for example your custom test color) and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Pixels you mark are kept in memory only and are gone when you close the app. Uninstalling the app removes the settings and the log. The log never contains file names or file contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
