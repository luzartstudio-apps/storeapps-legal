# HushMic — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

HushMic ("the app") is a Windows app that mutes and unmutes your microphones with a keyboard shortcut.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and it never connects to the internet.

**Microphone:** the app never records, listens to or opens an audio stream from your microphone. It only reads and changes the mute switch of your microphones and reads their names, using the standard Windows audio settings.

**Speakers (Pro):** if you switch speakers or headphones, the app changes the Windows default playback device. Nothing else about your audio is read or changed.

**Keyboard:** the shortcut is registered with Windows as a hotkey. The app is only told when that exact shortcut is pressed; it does not see anything else you type.

**Local data:** your shortcut, display options and a small diagnostic log (app start and mute/unmute events) are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Start with Windows:** the app starts automatically when you sign in only if you turn this on in the app. You can turn it off in the app or in Windows Settings.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
