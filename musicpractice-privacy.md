# Riffwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Riffwell ("the app") is a Windows app for practicing music: it plays audio files on your PC slower or faster without changing the pitch, changes the key, loops a part of a song and has a metronome.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app opens only the audio or video files you choose (with the file picker, drag and drop, paste or "Open with"). They are decoded and played on your PC using the audio codecs built into Windows; nothing is uploaded. The original files are never changed. If you use Export (Pro), the app writes a new file next to the original (or in your Music folder if that folder cannot be written) with a new name, and never overwrites an existing file.

**Practice data:** for each song you open, the app remembers your markers (and the names you type for them), the A–B loop, the speed, the key and where you stopped listening. This is saved in your Windows user profile (AppData) under a file name made from a one-way hash of the file's location, so the song's name and folder are not written there. Markers stay on your PC.

**Microphone:** the app does not use the microphone and never opens a recording device. It only plays sound through your speakers or headphones.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings (metronome tempo, speed trainer and export choices), the practice data described above and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains file names, folder names or file contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
