# Findwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

Findwell ("the app") is a Windows app that searches the files on your PC by name, by the words inside documents and, with the optional Pro upgrade, by the text in images.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no telemetry, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files and the index:** to make search fast, the app reads the file names on your drives and the text inside documents in the folders you choose, and builds an index from them. The index (file names, text extracted from documents and text recognized in images) is stored only on your PC, in the app's local data folder in your Windows user profile (LocalAppData). It is never uploaded or shared. The Privacy page shows where the index is and how much space it uses; you can exclude folders on the Locations page and remove the whole index at any time with "Delete index". Uninstalling the app deletes the index. The app only reads your files; it never changes, moves or deletes them.

**Text in images:** with Pro, or when you click "Read text in this image" on the free version, images and scanned PDFs are read with Windows' built-in text recognition (OCR), which runs entirely on your PC. The recognized text is kept only in the local index.

**Open history:** to rank results, the app can remember which files you open through it. This history is stored only on your PC. You can turn it off or clear it at any time on the Privacy page.

**Screen and keyboard:** the app never captures your screen and never records your keystrokes. The search shortcut (Alt+Shift+Space by default) is registered with Windows as a hotkey; the app does not watch any other keys.

**Network:** search works with Wi-Fi turned off. The app's network use is limited to: (1) the Microsoft Store checking your Pro license; and (2) only if you have Pro and turn on "Search by meaning (beta)" in Options, a one-time download of the language model files (about 118 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) over HTTPS. That download sends a normal web request for fixed public files; it never includes your files, file names, searches or any other personal data. The model then runs entirely on your PC. You can remove it at any time in Options.

**Local data:** your settings and a small diagnostic log are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log never contains file names, paths, search queries or file contents, only counts, timings and error codes. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
