# Subwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Subwell ("the app") is a Windows app for keeping track of your subscriptions: you type in each plan by hand and the app shows your totals, upcoming renewals and reminders.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store on our side, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your subscription list:** the plans you enter (name, amount, currency, billing cycle, renewal date, status, group, notes and a cancellation link) and the exchange rates you enter are saved only on your PC, in a data file in your Windows user profile (AppData). They are never sent anywhere. The app does not connect to any bank, does not ask for card numbers, account numbers or PINs, does not read your email and does not read bank statements. Please do not put card or bank details in the notes field.

**Your files:** the app reads or writes other files only when you choose them in a file dialog: exporting or importing a CSV list, and backing up or restoring your list.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not connect to the internet. If you add a cancellation link, opening it starts your default web browser and that website's own privacy policy applies. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores your list, its settings, a safety copy made before a restore, and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains your plan names, file names or file contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
