# StepTrail — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

StepTrail ("the app") is a Windows app that records the clicks you make while doing a task and turns them into a step-by-step guide with screenshots.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and it never connects to the internet.

**What the app records, and when:** only while you are recording — after you press "Start recording" or the start shortcut, and while the recording bar is on screen. During a recording, for each mouse click the app:
- takes a screenshot of the monitor under the pointer;
- reads the name and type of the item you clicked (for example "Save button") through Windows UI Automation, to write the step text;
- notes the name and window title of the app you clicked in, and the time.

Recording never starts on its own, the app does not run at startup, and you can pause at any time from the bar or with a shortcut. Clicks on StepTrail's own windows are not recorded.

**Typing:** the app does not watch the keyboard and never records what you type. When you click into a password field, the step only says "Click in the password box".

**Screenshots may show what is on your screen:** a screenshot contains whatever is visible at the moment of the click, which can include private information. Pause the recording before working with private information, and use "Black out area" (or "Blur area" in Pro) before you share a guide. Black-out and blur areas are applied to exported files; the original screenshots stay in the app's folder so you can change them.

**Where your data is stored:** recordings (screenshots and step text), your settings and a small diagnostic log are stored only on your PC, in the app's private data folder in your Windows user profile. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The diagnostic log never contains screenshots, step text or window titles. You can delete any recording, or all recordings, from within the app.

**Sharing:** files you export (HTML, PDF, Word, Markdown) or copy to the clipboard are created only when you choose to, and you decide where they go. StepTrail never sends them anywhere.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
