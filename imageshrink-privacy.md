# SnugPic — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

SnugPic ("the app") is a Windows app that compresses photos and images on your computer to a file size you choose.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app only opens the photos you drop, paste, choose or open with it. They are read and compressed only on your device. The smaller copy is saved next to the original photo (or in a folder you choose in the Pro version); your original photo is never changed. By default the copy keeps the photo's own information (such as the date taken, camera model and, if the photo had it, its GPS location), just like the original; the Pro option "Remove photo info" leaves all of it out. A photo you paste from the clipboard is first saved as a temporary image in the app's own folder in your user profile (deleted automatically after one day), and its small copy is saved in your Pictures\SnugPic folder. No photo, file name or photo information is ever uploaded.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection to work and does not send anything over the network. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them. The log never contains file names, folder paths or photo contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
