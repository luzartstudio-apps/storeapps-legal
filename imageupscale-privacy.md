# Crispwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Crispwell ("the app") is a Windows app that makes small or blurry pictures 2×, 4× or 8× larger and sharper with an AI model that runs on your PC.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no telemetry, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your images:** the images you open (file picker, drag-and-drop, Ctrl+V or "Open with") or paste from the clipboard are processed entirely on your PC by AI models that are included in the app. They are never uploaded or shared. The app only reads your images; it never changes, moves or deletes them. Each result is saved as a new file next to the original (for example "photo (4x).png") or in a folder you choose; pasted images are saved in your Pictures folder. An existing file is never overwritten. If you turn on "Keep photo info" (Pro), the photo information of a JPEG original (for example camera model, date and, if present, location) is copied into the new JPEG file; it is off by default.

**AI-generated detail:** the upscaling model adds detail that was not in the original picture. This detail is created by AI and may not match what was really there. If you see an AI result that is wrong or inappropriate, use "Report AI result" in the app (it opens your email app) or write to the address below.

**Network:** the app works fully offline and makes no network connections of its own. Its only network use is the Microsoft Store checking your Pro license.

**Local data:** your settings (chosen scale, image type, output format and folder, daily free count) and a small diagnostic log are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log never contains file names, paths or image content, only image sizes, timings and error codes. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Purchases:** the optional "Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
