# Rosterwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Rosterwell ("the app") is a Windows mail merge app: you open a spreadsheet and design one page, and the app creates PDF documents such as certificates, invitations or name badges from each row.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app reads only the files you choose: a spreadsheet (.xlsx or CSV), a background image, and, if you use the optional photo feature, photo files named in your spreadsheet, looked up next to the spreadsheet or in a folder you pick. Your spreadsheet may contain names or other details about people; these are processed only on this PC to create your PDFs and are never sent anywhere. The app never changes your spreadsheet or your images. It writes a PDF only when you export one, to the location you pick, and never overwrites an existing file.

**Network:** the app does not need or use an internet connection. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores its settings, your page designs (including any text you typed into them and a copy of the background images you chose) and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). The contents of your spreadsheet are not saved there. Uninstalling the app removes all of this. The log never contains file names, folder paths or the contents of your files: only counts and error codes.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
