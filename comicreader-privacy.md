# Panelwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Panelwell ("the app") is a Windows app for reading comic books and manga that are already on your computer (CBZ, CBR, CB7, CBT, ZIP, RAR, 7Z, PDF and folders of images).

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your files:** the app only opens the files and folders you choose, drag in, open with it, or add as library folders. They are read only on your device and are never changed, moved or uploaded. To show your library, the app looks through the library folders you added for comic files and folders of images. Some archives (for example most CB7/7z files) are unpacked page by page into a temporary folder inside the app's own data folder while the book is open; that folder is deleted when you close the book (or the next time the app starts). The optional scan cleanup in Pro only changes how pages look on screen, never the files.

**Network:** the app does not need an internet connection and does not send anything over the network. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** the app stores the following in your Windows user profile (AppData), only on this PC: its settings (including the library folders you added), your reading progress for each book you opened (the file's location and size, the page you reached, the reading mode and direction, and in Pro your bookmarks and collections), small cover thumbnails of the books in your library, and a small diagnostic log. You can clear the cover thumbnails in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. The log never contains file names, folder paths or page contents.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
