# Centwell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Centwell ("the app") is a Windows checkbook register: you type in your own checks, deposits and payments, see a running balance and reconcile with your bank statement.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your checkbook data:** everything you enter (account names you choose, opening balances, transactions with their dates, payees, categories, memos, amounts and check numbers, scheduled transactions and reconciliation history) is saved only on this PC, in your Windows user profile (AppData). The app never asks for bank account numbers, card numbers, PINs, tax numbers or bank passwords, and never connects to a bank. It does not print checks and does not move money.

**Files you choose:** the app reads or writes a file only when you ask it to: exporting a CSV file, backing up or restoring your checkbook, or (Pro) importing a CSV, OFX or QFX statement file you downloaded from your bank. When importing, only dates, amounts, payee names, memos and check numbers are read; account numbers, bank and branch codes found in the file are skipped and never saved, and long card numbers in descriptions are masked to their last four digits. Your original files are never changed. Printing (Pro) sends your register only to the printer you pick.

**Password lock (Pro):** if you turn it on, the app stores only a salted hash of your password (PBKDF2-SHA256), never the password itself. The lock keeps other people who use your PC out of the app; it does not encrypt your checkbook file.

**Network:** the app does not need or use an internet connection. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** besides your checkbook file and its previous version, the app keeps its settings, safety copies made before a restore, and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). Uninstalling the app removes them, so back up first if you want to keep your checkbook. The log never contains payees, amounts, file names or folder paths — only counts and error codes.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
