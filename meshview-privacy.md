# MeshView — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

MeshView ("the app") is a Windows app for viewing, measuring and checking 3D print models (STL, 3MF and OBJ files).

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and it never connects to the internet.

**Your model files:** the app only reads the files you open — by double-clicking them, dragging them onto the window or choosing them in the Open dialog. To let you flip through a folder with the arrow keys, it lists the other .stl, .3mf and .obj files in the same folder; it does not open them until you move to them. Model files are read on your PC and are never uploaded, copied elsewhere or modified. Pictures of the view are only saved where you choose, or copied to the clipboard when you ask.

**Local data:** your display choices (units, colors, background, panel layout, print estimate settings), the list of recently opened files and a small diagnostic log are stored in your Windows user profile (AppData). The log records technical events such as how long a model took to open; it never contains model contents. You can clear the recent files list in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

**Purchases:** the optional "MeshView Pro" upgrade is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
