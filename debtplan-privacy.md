# Owewell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Owewell ("the app") is a Windows debt payoff planner: you type in your debts and a monthly budget, and the app calculates a payoff plan. It is for calculation only and is not financial advice.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your plan data:** everything you enter (names you choose for your debts, balances, interest rates, minimum payments, your monthly budget, extra payments and the amounts you record as paid) is saved only on this PC, in your Windows user profile (AppData). The app never asks for bank account numbers, card numbers, PINs or bank passwords, and never connects to a bank. It does not lend money, does not recommend lenders and does not move money.

**Files you choose:** the app writes a file only when you ask it to export your schedule as PDF or CSV (Pro), and only to the location you pick. It does not open or read any other files of yours.

**Network:** the app does not need or use an internet connection. Purchases of the optional "Pro" upgrade are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); we never see your payment details.

**Local data:** besides your plan file and its previous version, the app keeps its settings and a small diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (AppData). If a plan file is ever damaged, it is kept next to the plan under a new name rather than overwritten. Uninstalling the app removes all of this. The log never contains debt names, amounts, file names or folder paths: only counts and error codes.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
